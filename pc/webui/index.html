For phones without the app, such as an iPhone.

Open on the phone

  1. Scan this with the phone's camera. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as this PC.
  2. The browser warns about the certificate once. On iPhone: Show Details, then visit this website.
  3. Pair the controller with the phone over Bluetooth, then press Start link.

The link works until LudusCtrl is closed; the next start makes a new one.
